Is this the actual laptop?
It is the actual drive. The laptop itself is still sitting on a counter in a strip mall under a sticky note with the wrong name on it, and the shop stopped calling somewhere around the third year.
Why did you never go back for it?
The week got busy and then the year did. By the time it came back to me, three separate committees had read the whole thing end to end and one of them had printed it as a hardcover with an index.
Are you ever going to sort the dust bath photographs?
No. Sorting them would mean deciding that some of them are worse than the others, and i am not prepared to insult the dirt like that in writing.
Do you actually have a job?
i have a board seat, a gallery, a tax notice, a pardon and a folder of nine hundred hours of footage. At no point anywhere in that sentence does a job appear, and nobody has ever brought it up.
What happens when i buy?
